Ancient Numismatics

Explore the world of ancient coinage - from the riverbeds of Lydia where electrum was first gathered for coinage, to Greek, Persian, Roman, Byzantine and many other civilizations. Learn about the history, metals, designs, and collecting traditions behind some of the most fascinating objects of antiquity.

About

Welcome to my site. This project is dedicated to helping collectors and enthusiasts better understand coinage, history, and how to get started.

I created this site to organize information in a way that’s easy to follow, whether you're brand new or already experienced.

Over time, I’ll continue adding guides, resources, and tools to make this a valuable reference.

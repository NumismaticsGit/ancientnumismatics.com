Ancient Numismatics

Explore the world of ancient coinage - from the riverbeds of Lydia where electrum was first gathered for coinage, to Greek, Persian, Roman, Byzantine and many other civilizations. Learn about the history, metals, designs, and collecting traditions behind some of the most fascinating objects of antiquity.

About

This site is designed as a starting point for anyone interested in ancient coinage, whether you're completely new or already involved in numismatics.

Ancient coinage can feel overwhelming at first - different cultures, unfamiliar designs, and a wide range of historical context. The goal here is to simplify that process and provide a clear path into the field.

This project takes a more modern approach to learning, focusing on clarity, organization, and ease of navigation. Instead of requiring deep prior knowledge, the information is presented in a way that is easier to explore, understand, and build upon over time.

Most of the content is geared toward beginner and intermediate collectors, covering the foundational knowledge needed to confidently explore, collect, and understand ancient coins, while also including more advanced topics where they add useful depth.

The goal is to make ancient numismatics more accessible for a new generation of collectors, while still serving as a reliable and structured resource for those looking to deepen their understanding.
